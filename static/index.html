Field manual

Select a goal, then double-click

Choose move or attack. A single click selects a destination or target and shows a path plus the order details below the map. Double-click the same goal to execute. There are no confirmation popups. Escape cancels; Enter executes a selected goal. Doors and ladders can also be double-clicked.

Shotgun and medical support

The shotgun delivers 10 damage within 5 tiles. A medikit takes a support slot and provides two treatments, each restoring up to 6 HP for 1 AP. Equip it, then double-click an adjacent wounded teammate. It cannot revive the dead or heal through walls.

Facing, peeking and corner throws

The direction-arrow icon lets you face a map point for 0 AP, even after your action points are spent. Facing rotates the model and its heading marker; squad awareness still checks line of sight in every direction.

A peek icon appears beside usable cover corners. Peeking costs 1 AP: briefly step past the edge, look, then retreat. Every eligible enemy can react with one shot, capped at a 10% hit chance. Prone fighters cannot peek. Frag and smoke grenades can turn the corner from directly beside its edge, using a clear second throw segment within their total range. Peek or explore the destination first.

Last-seen contacts

Gray silhouettes and gray minimap markers show a contact’s last observed position and round. They are uncertain memories, not live enemy positions. Reacquiring an enemy removes the gray marker; moving close enough to inspect its old location clears it as well.

Mission equipment

Choose a theme and map size before deployment. Click a pictured equipment slot to browse the catalog. Each fighter takes a main weapon, M9 and two different support items. Reload costs 1 AP. Handgun fire costs 1 AP; rifles and explosives end the turn. M24 shots and overwatch require 2 AP. Automatic fire uses up to 3 rounds with −20 aim.

Sight and explosives

Every weapon can target an empty visible point within range. Walls, cover and smoke block the firing fighter’s sight. Friendly fire is possible. Smoke lasts 3 hostile phases. Demolition charges must be planted within 1.5 tiles and detonate after 2 hostile phases with a 4-tile radius. Withdraw before detonation.

Buildings and stances

Open or close adjacent doors and windows for 1 AP. Windows cannot be climbed. Use cutaway views to see rooms; ladders and stairs connect floors. Kneeling and prone stances reduce movement speed, visibility and incoming accuracy. Prone fighters cannot climb or fire RPGs. Structural health appears above buildings and vehicles; destroyed structures become rubble. Collapse kills upper-floor occupants.

Overview and controls

The minimap shows explored terrain, detected contacts, and gray last-seen markers. Click it to move the camera. Right-drag orbits, left-drag pans, and the wheel zooms. WebGPU is preferred, with WebGL 2 as a fallback. Hover icons for their function; all controls have accessible labels. Sound starts after your first click; there is no music.
